# Changelog

All notable changes to OfferGuard are documented here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-21
### Added
- Offer checker: paste any job post/message/offer letter — 15 weighted scam-pattern detectors (fee & deposit demands, documents-before-offer, task/recharge scams, overseas-agent traps, government-job impersonation, guaranteed selection, unrealistic pay for low-skill work, WhatsApp/Telegram-only hiring, crypto salary, MLM, urgency, secrecy, shortened links, data-harvest forms) with a 0–100 risk score, verdict and next steps. Combo rules escalate the worst patterns (fee+documents, task+crypto, overseas+fee) to CRITICAL.
- Recruiter/domain analysis: extracts emails and websites, flags free-mail recruiters, and detects look-alike domains against a built-in table of official domains for major Indian employers and government bodies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, India Post, SSC, UPSC, Railways, SBI…).
- Guided 8-step verification checklist with official careers-page search and MCA registry links; printable verification card.
- Overseas-agent module: eMigrate registration, ₹30,000 + GST legal fee cap, work-vs-tourist visa rule, passport safety, ECR clearance, MEA helpline 1800-11-3090.
- Practice drills: 3 scripted scams (WhatsApp fresher offer, fake government selection letter, overseas "Thailand IT job" funnel) with scored choices and explanations.
- Application tracker with statuses (applied / interview / offer / verified genuine / rejected / scam).
- Help & report flow (1930, bank, cybercrime.gov.in, identity-theft steps) plus an on-device evidence locker with text export.
- Full UI in English, हिन्दी (Hindi) and മലയാളം (Malayalam), including drills and checker explanations.
- Dark mode, offline PWA, Android APK + iOS build workflows, GitHub Pages site. No account, no server, no tracking.
